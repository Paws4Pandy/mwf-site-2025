## **Why Cherry Valley is a Special Place to Call Home**

Cherry Valley is a peaceful rural village in PEC prized for its rolling farmland, tight-knit community, and proximity to outdoor adventures like Sandbanks Provincial Park. It balances quiet country living with vibrant cultural events and artisan spirit, appealing to families, retirees, and nature enthusiasts.

## **What You’ll Love About Living in Cherry Valley**

* Natural Beauty: Surrounded by farmland, forest, and lakes, Cherry Valley offers a picturesque retreat from city life.  
* Community Events: The village buzzes with seasonal gatherings such as the Cherry Valley Strawberry Social and First Fridays, which feature local music, gallery openings, and fine dining.  
* Artisan Culture: Local artisans and craftspeople thrive here, with galleries and boutiques displaying pottery, photography, and handmade goods.  
* Proximity to Parks: Close to Sandbanks Provincial Park’s famous beaches and hiking spots like Lake on the Mountain Provincial Park.  
* Rural Lifestyle with Conveniences: While offering quiet country living, Cherry Valley is within driving distance of Picton, Belleville, and Waupoos for shopping and services.

## **Recommended Local Spots in Cherry Valley**

Dining & Local Shopping:

* [The Store in Cherry Valley](https://visitthecounty.com/listing/store-cherry-valley) – The quintessential general store offering local produce, gifts, and camping essentials.  
* [Hart House](https://hart.house/) – Elegant lodging with Italian-strength coffee and stunning East Lake views.  
* Nearby Picton and Waupoos offer world-class dining and wineries just a short drive away.

Outdoor Attractions & Parks:

* [Sandbanks Provincial Park](https://www.ontarioparks.com/park/sandbanks) – One of Canada’s premier beach destinations with expansive sandy beaches and family-friendly amenities.  
* [Lake on the Mountain Provincial Park](https://www.ontarioparks.com/park/lakeonthemountain) – Unique elevated freshwater lake with panoramic views and walking trails.  
* Cherry Valley Lions Memorial Park – Free boat launch, picnic spots, and camping near the village center.

Culture & Events:

* [Cherry Valley Strawberry Social](https://visitthecounty.com/event/666c3807ec1223400cda3ad1) – An annual beloved event celebrating local produce and community camaraderie.  
* First Friday events featuring local crafts, music, and open galleries.
